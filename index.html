Life in the 2000s CyberCafe Era 🌐

Posted by Admin_Nostalgia on Thursday, November 16, 2006

1. Renting ₱15/Hour to Play GunBound, Ragnarok, & Counter-Strike

Back in 2005, nothing beats sprinting to the nearest Internet Cafe right after the school bell rings. We’d hand over ₱15 coins to the computer attendant for 1 hour of PC time. We spent hours grinding on Ragnarok Online, aiming wind shots in GunBound, or shouting across the room during Counter-Strike 1.6 LAN matches ("No Flash!").

2. Customizing HTML & Music on Profile Pages

Everyone was a web developer in 2008 without realizing it! Learning CSS & HTML tags just to change background colors, add autoplay background music (MIDI or MP3), cursor glitter trails, and layout themes on social profiles.
